Frequently asked常见问题

Which Macs can run ffilm?哪些 Mac 能用？

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.搭载 Apple 芯片、系统为 macOS 26 及以上的 Mac。

How do I install the Mac version?Mac 版怎么安装？

Open the downloaded ffilm.dmg and drag ffilm into Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally.打开下载的 ffilm.dmg，把 ffilm 拖进“应用程序”即可。App 已通过苹果签名与公证，可以直接打开。

Which file formats are supported?支持哪些格式？

Camera RAW files supported by Apple’s system, HEIC and JPEG.苹果系统支持的相机 RAW、HEIC 和 JPEG。

What’s the difference between Film and Edit?胶片模式和修图模式有什么区别？

Film mode develops your photo through a simulated film and paper. Edit mode skips the film and lets you grade the original directly with filters and adjustments.胶片模式让照片经过模拟的胶片和相纸成像；修图模式不套胶片，直接用滤镜和各项调整修原片。

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?照片会被上传吗？

No. Everything is processed on your device. See the Privacy Policy.不会，全部在你的设备上处理。详见隐私政策。

Does ffilm look exactly like real film?和真实胶片一模一样吗？

ffilm simulates the look of film based on published technical data. Results differ from real prints, which also vary by lab and scanner.ffilm 依据公开的技术资料模拟胶片效果，与实际冲印会有差别；真实冲印本身也会因冲印店和扫描仪而不同。
