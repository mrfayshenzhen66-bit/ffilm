Privacy Policy隐私政策

ffilm doesn’t collect your data.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the ffilm apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad, made by FEI FEI.

Your photos

Photos you open in ffilm are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to any server, and ffilm has no account system.

Data collection

ffilm does not collect, store or share any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code.

Permissions

  • iPhone and iPad: to export, ffilm asks for “Add to Photos” access only. It cannot read the photos in your library. Photos you import are chosen by you in the system picker.
  • Mac: ffilm reads only the files and folders you open, and writes only where you choose to export.

Settings stored on your device

Your edits, presets and preferences are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

ffilm collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

ffilm 不收集你的数据。

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

本政策适用于 FEI FEI 开发的 ffilm（Mac、iPhone 与 iPad 版）。

你的照片

在 ffilm 中打开的照片全部在你的设备上处理，不会上传到任何服务器。ffilm 没有账号系统。

数据收集

ffilm 不收集、不存储、不分享任何个人数据，不含统计分析、广告或第三方追踪代码。

权限

  • iPhone 与 iPad：导出时只申请“添加到相册”权限，读不到你相册里的照片。导入的照片由你在系统选择器中选取。
  • Mac：ffilm 只读取你打开的文件和文件夹，只写入你选择的导出位置。

保存在设备上的设置

你的调整、配方和偏好设置保存在本机，方便下次接着用。删除 App 即一并删除。

儿童

ffilm 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更

本政策如有变更，会在此页面发布新版本并更新日期。
